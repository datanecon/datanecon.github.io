Our paper *Exclusive Secrets*, with Giacomo Calzolari and Alireza Naghavi, on how weak trade-secret protection narrows suppliers' buyer networks, was accepted at the [*Journal of the European Economic Association*](https://academic.oup.com/jeea/advance-article/doi/10.1093/jeea/jvag054/8785138). We summarise the findings in a [VoxEU column](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/spilling-secrets-and-shrinking-chains-how-weak-laws-narrow-supply-networks).
