Together with Giacomo Calzolari and Alireza Naghavi, I have written a column for VoxEU/CEPR based on our paper *Exclusive Secrets*, forthcoming in the *Journal of the European Economic Association*.

The column sets out, in plain language, why weak trade-secret protection leads manufacturers to keep their suppliers exclusive, and what that means for the shape of global supply networks. The evidence comes from worldwide automotive-parts contracts.

You can read it here: [Spilling secrets and shrinking chains: how weak laws narrow supply networks](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/spilling-secrets-and-shrinking-chains-how-weak-laws-narrow-supply-networks).
