I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at [SKEMA Business School](https://www.skema.edu/en/faculty-and-research/professors/david-angenendt), based at the Sophia Antipolis campus and a member of the Knowledge, Technology and Organization (KTO) research centre. My research lies at the intersection of innovation economics, intellectual property, and industrial organisation. In current projects, I study trade secrets and vertical contracts, patent and disclosure policy, the measurement of innovation using patent data, and firms' engagement with universities.

I am also a Research Associate at the [Centre for Business Research](https://www.jbs.cam.ac.uk/centres/business-research-cbr/people/research-associates/) at the University of Cambridge. Before joining SKEMA, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher and Lecturer at the Technical University of Munich. I received my PhD in Economics from the [University of Bologna](https://dse.unibo.it/en) in 2019.
